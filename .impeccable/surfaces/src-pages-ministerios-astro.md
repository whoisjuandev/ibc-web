# Ministerios (src/pages/ministerios.astro)

Scope: the ministries page. Mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led. It extends the established world recorded in DESIGN.md; no new direction was chosen.

Audience and job: a parent, a teenager or a young adult checking whether there is something for their age, and when. Action: come to the right weekly gathering, or ask about a Grupo de vida.

Structure: page hero, "Encuentros 4G" as a filtered week row in age order, a text-only black panel for Grupos de vida, the remaining ministries as cards over a black-and-white photo, closing contact.

Constraints: ages and times come from the church's own Instagram highlight. Leaders' names, addresses and phone numbers from the Grupos de vida highlight are not published. No group photos yet, so the age groups use the typographic week cards.

Unresolved: recent photos of each age group; whether to publish the Grupos de vida list.
